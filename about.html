A house of three quiet wellness brands.

Adwitiya means "the one without a second." We took the name carefully — and we have tried to live up to it slowly, season by season, since 2017.

Our story

Begun by hand. Kept that way.

What began as a single small room above an old apothecary in Rajkot has grown — gently — into a family of three wellness houses, fifteen rooms, and forty-two trained hands.

We did not set out to build a chain. We set out to perfect a few rituals, source a few good oils, and to keep welcoming our guests by name. Nine years later, the rituals are sharper, the oils are local, and we still know most of our guests by name.

Each of our houses — Ganga, Amore and Satva — keeps its own room, its own colour and its own quiet language. What they share is how they are made: slowly, in Gujarat, by certified therapists who were born to these traditions.

What we hold to

Mission & quiet vision.

Mission

To return the body to itself.

We exist to deliver wellness that is honest, restorative and uncomplicated — without resorting to gimmicks, packaged science, or pressure to buy more than you need.

One room, one ritual, one hour at a time. Every guest leaves a little lighter than they arrived.

Vision

A quieter Indian wellness.

To become India's most considered house of wellness brands — known not for our scale, but for the standard we hold every single room to, every single day.

We will grow when it is right. We will not grow before then.

A note from the founder
“

I built Adwitiya because I wanted somewhere my own mother would go, and feel looked after.

I trained in Kerala, apprenticed in Bali, and came home to Rajkot to find that the women I'd grown up with — my mother, her sisters, our neighbours — still had nowhere local that felt right. So we made one. Then another. Then a third.

We will keep making rooms like this for as long as the work is honest. Thank you for trusting us with your hour.

Tara Borah
Founder & Creative Director
