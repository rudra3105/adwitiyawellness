The house

Wellness, as a practice.

Sattva is our quietest, most clinical house. Not a spa — a counsel. Built for people who want a practice, not a ritual; for the body that has to last another forty years.

We sit with you. We listen. We draw up a small plan — six weeks of breath, movement and warmth — and we check on you, gently, when the six weeks are up.

Our team includes two BAMS-certified ayurvedic practitioners, three yoga teachers trained in the Iyengar lineage, and a quiet, attentive front-of-house who remembers everything.
