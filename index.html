About Adwitiya

Wellness, kept simple, kept honest.

For nearly a decade, Adwitiya has built a small family of spa and wellness houses on a single belief — that good rituals, sourced ingredients and the right hands are enough.

Our therapists are trained and certified across the North East, our oils are pressed close to home, and every room is dressed by hand. We grow slowly, on purpose.
