A note on who we hire

We only hire North East professional and certified staff.

The traditions our houses are built on belong to this region. We honour them by hiring locally — therapists who trained in our schools, in our languages, and who carry these practices home with them every night. If that's you, please apply.

Why join us

A house, not a hustle.

Most spas burn through people. We are deliberately the opposite. Our average tenure is six years and we have never lost a senior therapist we wanted to keep.

Hiring process

A slow, respectful conversation.

From application to first day, four to six weeks. We will never ghost you, and we will tell you why if it isn't a fit.

All applicants receive
  • · A written reply within 7 days
  • · A travel stipend for the trial day
  • · Honest feedback either way
  1. Step 01 · day 1

    Apply

    Send us the form below, or write to us directly. CV is welcome but optional — many of our best therapists trained traditionally.

  2. Step 02 · day 5–7

    A short call

    Twenty minutes with one of our house managers — in your language, at a time that suits you.

  3. Step 03 · week 2

    A trial day

    Spend a paid day with the team — shadow a therapist, share a meal, demonstrate one technique. Travel is on us.

  4. Step 04 · week 3

    A small offer

    A written offer with full terms. Take a week to think. We have never rushed anyone.

  5. Step 05 · week 4–6

    First room

    Two weeks of paid onboarding, then your first room. We pair you with a senior therapist for the first month.
