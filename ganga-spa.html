The house

A practice built on the water.

Ganga is our oldest house — the room where it all started. We named it for the river because the river is what taught us to slow down.

Every Ganga ritual moves the way water moves — long, even, never hurried. Our therapists train for two full years before they take their first guest unaccompanied. Most of them have been with us for five.

The room is dressed in stone, linen and the smell of warmed almond. The music is the river, curated for this room.
