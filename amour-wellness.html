The house

The room you book for two.

Amore is our softer house — built around the idea that wellness is best when shared. Our rooms are paired, our oils are gentler, and our timing is built for couples who want to whisper.

We opened Amore in Rajkot in 2019 after too many guests at Ganga asked if they could bring their partner. The answer is yes — and the room is purpose-built for it.

Each visit ends the same way: tea by the window, in silence, for as long as you'd like. Most stay an hour. Some stay until the room closes.
